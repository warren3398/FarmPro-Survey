FarmPro Survey v6 — Continuous Kobo-style Form

• Uses the uploaded KoboToolbox XLSForm questionnaire.
• Interview is now one continuous scrolling form instead of one-question-at-a-time.
• Questions are organized under collapsible section headers.
• Existing relevance/skip logic remains active.
• Save Draft, Resume Draft, required-field validation, offline local storage, and CSV export are retained.
• Floating Save and Back-to-Top controls are included for long interviews.
• For installable PWA behavior, serve through HTTPS or localhost.
